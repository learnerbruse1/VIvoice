# Ethics and responsible use

VIvoice can transform voices and, with an imported voice model, change the
speaker's identity. That power carries real-world risks, so use it responsibly.

## Requirements

1. **Consent first.** Only clone or imitate a voice when the speaker has given
   informed consent. "Publicly available" audio is not consent.
2. **Do not impersonate.** Never present a converted voice as another real
   person without their explicit permission, and never use it to deceive,
   defraud, or harm.
3. **Disclose synthetic media where appropriate.** If output could plausibly be
   mistaken for a real person's voice, label it as processed.
4. **Respect model licenses.** Voice models, base models, and training data have
   their own licenses and terms. Importing a model does not transfer those
   obligations to you-free; comply with the source's terms.
5. **Do not process minors' or third parties' voices without the relevant
   consent.**

## Prohibited uses

- Fraud, scams, or bypassing voice authentication
- Defamation, harassment, or producing non-consensual content
- Anything unlawful in your jurisdiction
